PART 2 — SEPARATE CURRENT APPLICABILITY

Add a distinct current-applicability validation layer.

For a historical human decision to remain applicable to a newer workbook, validate:

1. the historical decision itself authenticates successfully;
2. the reviewed source term still exists;
3. all mapping rows directly governing that reviewed source term are compared old→current;
4. relevant dependencies represented in the original packet are compared old→current;
5. each semantic transition between workbook versions is governed;
6. any changed dependency is classified as:
   - NON_MATERIAL_TO_DECISION
   - MATERIAL_REQUIRES_REOPEN
7. only explicit governed NON_MATERIAL determinations allow carry-forward.

Do not infer non-materiality merely because the changed row has a different source IRI.
