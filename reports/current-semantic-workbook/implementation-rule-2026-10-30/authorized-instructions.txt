Its intended behavior is:

```sparql

  ?procedure ssn:implementedBy ?system .
  ?system ssn:implements ?procedure .
}
WHERE {
  ?procedure cco:prescribes ?execution .
  ?execution cco:has_agent ?system .
}
```
