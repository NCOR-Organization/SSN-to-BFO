## Approved changes

**1. `implementedBy` / `implements` — two rows**

Use the same semantic justification for both inverse properties.

Explain that:

- `implementedBy` relates a Procedure to its implementing System; `implements` expresses the inverse.
- The SSN source restriction `Procedure ⊑ ∀implementedBy.System` supports classifying implementers as Systems.
- The process-mediated interpretation treats a System's participation as agent in a process prescribed by a Procedure as sufficient for inferring the implementation relation.
- This does not identify implementation with a direct CCO prescription relation between the Procedure and System.
- The process-mediated condition is sufficient, not necessary; an implementation relation does not imply that a particular execution has occurred.

Preserve the distinction between the retained OWL domain axiom and external-rule coverage. Keep implementation mechanics and OWL 2 DL diagnostics in their existing documentation.
