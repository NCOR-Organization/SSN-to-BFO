## Explicit human decision

Adopt the following formal OWL typing axioms in the current SSN/SOSA mapping:
```yaml
sosa:observedProperty rdfs:domain sosa:Observation
sosa:observedProperty rdfs:range sosa:ObservableProperty
```

Do not restore the previously removed:
```yaml
sosa:observedProperty rdfs:subPropertyOf cco:has_input
```

Do not introduce another BFO, CCO, or RO superproperty.

## Rationale

`sosa:observedProperty` relates an Observation to the ObservableProperty it observes.

No satisfactory existing BFO/CCO/RO superproperty has been identified that captures this relationship without introducing unwanted participation or input semantics.

ObservableProperties can be specifically dependent continuants or occurrent process profiles. The existing `sosa:ObservableProperty` class mapping accommodates both.

The new source-class domain and range axioms make classifications logically enforceable through OWL inference. They do not, by themselves, establish that the property belongs to the same FeatureOfInterest associated with the Observation.

These are global OWL typing commitments, not SHACL validation constraints.
