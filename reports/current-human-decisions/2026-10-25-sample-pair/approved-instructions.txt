SEMANTIC DECISION — hasSample

Use the following approved substance in the decision rationale:

`sosa:hasSample` relates a Feature of Interest to a Sample used to represent it. No direct BFO/CCO mapping is adopted.

The reviewed direct candidates do not preserve the source meaning generally:

- Mapping to CCO `represented by` would treat the Sample endpoint as representational information content, which does not capture physical specimens, sampling locations, and other material Samples generally.
- Mapping to BFO `generically depends on` would assign the generically-dependent-content role to the sampled Feature of Interest rather than capturing samplehood.
- A participation mapping also does not represent the relation between a sampled feature and its sample.

Historical work proposed the composite representation path:

represented_by(feature, information-content)
AND
generically_depends_on(information-content, sample)
→
hasSample(feature, sample)

This path should NOT be reinstated as an unconditional formal mapping.

It is not sufficient for samplehood: an ordinary representational carrier such as a book, photograph, recording, or other information-bearing artifact may bear content representing an entity without thereby being a Sample of that entity.

It is also not established as necessary: SOSA's notion of intended representativeness does not by itself require commitment to a distinct CCO information-content entity borne by every Sample.

The previous OWL implementation additionally creates simplicity/profile problems because `hasSample` is inverse-functional and is the inverse of a property used with cardinality/functionality constraints. That implementation issue is secondary to the semantic decision and is NOT the reason for the no-direct outcome.

Accordingly:

No reviewed direct BFO/CCO relation or unconditional BFO/CCO relation chain adequately captures `sosa:hasSample` across its source usage.

This does NOT rule out:
- a future guarded representation rule;
- a query-level mapping;
- a more specialized sample-provenance model;
- weaker documentary correspondences to other vocabularies.

SEMANTIC DECISION — isSampleOf

Use the following approved substance in the decision rationale:

`sosa:isSampleOf` relates a Sample to the Feature of Interest that it is intended to be representative of. No direct BFO/CCO mapping is adopted.

The reviewed direct candidates do not preserve the source meaning generally:

- Mapping to CCO `represents` would treat every Sample as representational information content.
- Mapping to BFO `is carrier of` would treat the sampled Feature of Interest as generically dependent content.
- Mapping to participation would confuse the feature being sampled with a process in which the Sample participates.

Historical work proposed:

is_carrier_of(sample, information-content)
AND
represents(information-content, feature)
→
isSampleOf(sample, feature)

This should NOT be reinstated as an unconditional formal mapping.

The representation/carrier path is not sufficient for samplehood. A carrier may bear information representing an entity without being intended or functioning as a Sample of that entity.

It is also not established as necessary. Intended representativeness in SOSA does not by itself require a separately instantiated CCO information-content entity.

Because `isSampleOf` is functional, an overbroad representation-chain mapping would also create undesirable consequences: a carrier bearing representations of multiple distinct entities could infer multiple `isSampleOf` successors and thereby force equality of those represented entities.

The historical OWL chain also creates simplicity/profile conflicts with retained source functionality/cardinality axioms. Again, this is an implementation consequence, not the semantic basis of the no-direct decision.

Accordingly:

No reviewed direct BFO/CCO relation or unconditional BFO/CCO relation chain adequately captures `sosa:isSampleOf` across its source usage.

This does NOT rule out a future more specifically conditioned representation or provenance model.
