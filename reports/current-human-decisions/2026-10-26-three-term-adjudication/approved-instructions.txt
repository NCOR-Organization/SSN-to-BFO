## Decisions

### 1. `ssn:forProperty`

Current governed row:

- workbook: `mappings/SSN2BFO-COMS.xlsx`
- sheet/cell row: `Sheet2!49`
- RowID: `urn:uuid:287f1392-ca8f-4dc2-9d42-b60b3962adfc`
- current predicate: `owl:propertyChainAxiom`
- current target: `cco:described_by o cco:is_about`

New decision:

- classification: `final`
- disposition: `no_direct_mapping`
- remove the active BFO/CCO mapping:
  - clear predicate
  - clear target
- preserve the RowID
- replace/update the reasoning with the substantive new rationale below.

Rationale:

`ssn:forProperty` relates a Procedure or System to a Property that it acts on or observes. The proposed `cco:described_by o cco:is_about` chain establishes only that an entity is described by information that is about some entity; it does not entail that the described entity acts on or observes that Property. This is especially clear for the System branch of the source semantics: a physical System may be described by an information artifact that is about a Property without the System thereby observing or acting on that Property. No reviewed BFO/CCO relation uniformly captures the source relation across its Procedure and System uses. Retain `ssn:forProperty` as a source-native relation without a direct BFO/CCO mapping.

Do not remove or alter legitimate source-native hierarchy assertions such as `sosa:observes rdfs:subPropertyOf ssn:forProperty`.

### 2. `ssn-system:SystemProperty`

Current governed row:

- `Sheet1!45`
- RowID: `urn:uuid:9ea02b02-c86b-4a0b-a56b-47c1dea0bfa4`
- current predicate: `rdfs:subClassOf`
- current target:

`(bfo:SpecificallyDependentContinuant or bfo:ProcessProfile) and (cco:prescribed_by some cco:ArtifactFunctionSpecification)`

New decision:

- classification: `final`
- disposition: `inheritance_sufficient`
- clear the direct predicate and target
- preserve the RowID

Rationale:

`ssn-system:SystemProperty` is already a subclass of `ssn:Property` and therefore inherits the governed BFO-level treatment of `ssn:Property`. The source characterizes a SystemProperty as an identifiable and observable characteristic representing a System's ability to operate its primary purpose, but does not require every SystemProperty to be prescribed by an Artifact Function Specification. The restriction `cco:prescribed_by some cco:ArtifactFunctionSpecification` therefore introduces an unsupported existential and design-specification commitment. No additional direct class mapping is required beyond the inherited `ssn:Property` mapping.

Do not replace the current expression with an explicit direct `(SDC or ProcessProfile)` assertion; that typing should remain inherited.

### 3. `ssn-system:MeasurementRange`

Current governed row:

- `Sheet1!43`
- RowID: `urn:uuid:d180e765-d4d1-4578-a33a-6e96308e01db`
- current predicate: `rdfs:subClassOf`
- current target:

`bfo:Function and (bfo:has_realization some (sosa:Observation and (cco:caused_by some (ssn:Stimulus and (cco:prescribed_by some cco:ArtifactFunctionSpecification)))))`

New decision:

- classification: `final`
- disposition: `inheritance_sufficient`
- clear the direct predicate and target
- preserve the RowID

Rationale:

`ssn-system:MeasurementRange` is formally a subclass of `ssn-system:SystemProperty` and ultimately of `ssn:Property`, so it inherits the applicable governed BFO-level characterization through that hierarchy. Its natural-language definition describes “the set of values that the Sensor can return as the Result of an Observation under the defined Conditions,” which is ontologically awkward with its formal placement under `SystemProperty`. For this mapping, follow the formal source hierarchy and treat the “set of values” wording as imprecise shorthand for a system characteristic concerning the range of possible result values, rather than reinterpreting the class as an Information Content Entity.

The source does not justify identifying `MeasurementRange` itself as a `bfo:Function`, nor requiring it to have an Observation as a realization, an Observation caused by a Stimulus, or an Artifact Function Specification. The current Function/realization/causation expression conflates the system property describing the range with the function or processes through which measurement capability is exercised. No additional direct mapping is required beyond inherited treatment.

Do NOT broaden the governed mapping of `ssn:Property` to include `cco:InformationContentEntity`.
